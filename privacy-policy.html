How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, configure, and operate your AI voice agent system

We use trusted third-party providers to deliver our service, including payment processing (Paddle), voice and telephony infrastructure (Vapi, ElevenLabs, Twilio), and workflow automation (Make.com, GoHighLevel). These providers process data solely to support the functionality of our system and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations.

Data Retention

We retain client and prospect information only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by applicable law.

Your Rights
