What Velexia Does

Velexia is an autonomous AI voice agent system built for U.S. real estate brokerages. It replaces the traditional Inside Sales Agent (ISA) role: contacting inbound leads within 90 seconds, qualifying them, handling objections, and booking appointments automatically. Velexia is sold as a B2B SaaS deployment, not a consumer product.

Why the Public Website Is Access-Gated

velexia.co is intentionally gated behind a per-prospect access code. This is a deliberate business decision, not an attempt to obscure legitimacy: the sales page contains detailed competitive positioning, pricing methodology, and proprietary system demonstrations that are shared only with qualified prospects after a discovery call, to protect against competitor scraping in a small, high-value market (U.S. real estate brokerage software).
